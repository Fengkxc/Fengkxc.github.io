研究工作Publications

WinTA-GIL: Windowed Trajectory Alignment for GNSS/IMU/LiDAR Heading Refinement in Intermittent Signal Environments IROS 2026Oral
K. Feng, Z. Wen, Z. Liao, X. Xia, Y. Li*　·　一作 · 已录用（Oral）First author · Accepted (Oral)

将航向估计从单次初始对齐扩展为基于时间窗口的持续轨迹一致性优化，通过 LIO 与 GNSS 轨迹的加权刚体配准反解航向，并以自适应伪观测注入 EKF，显著抑制 GNSS 中断恢复阶段的航向漂移。 Reformulates heading estimation from one-shot alignment into a continuous window-based trajectory-consistency optimization; recovers heading via weighted rigid alignment between LIO and GNSS trajectories, injected as an adaptive EKF pseudo-observation to suppress heading drift after GNSS outages.
