论文Publications
WinTA-GIL: Window-based Trajectory Alignment for Heading Refinement in GNSS-Intermittent Environments IROS 2026
Kaixin Feng, Yu Zhang, ..., You Li

将航向估计从单次初始对齐扩展为基于时间窗口的持续轨迹一致性优化，通过 LIO 与 GNSS 轨迹的加权刚体配准反解航向，并以自适应伪观测注入 EKF，在 GNSS 中断恢复阶段显著抑制航向漂移。 Reformulates heading estimation from one-shot initial alignment into a continuous, window-based trajectory-consistency optimization; recovers heading via weighted rigid alignment between LIO and GNSS trajectories and injects it as an adaptive pseudo-observation into an EKF, strongly suppressing heading drift after GNSS outages.
